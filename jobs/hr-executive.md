# HR Executive – HRMS & Employee Services
`Full-time · Remote · Salary: negotiable, discussed at interview.`

### About the role
The HR Executive – HRMS & Employee Services is responsible for managing HRMS operations, employee system access, attendance and leave administration, and employee support services. This role ensures smooth HR system functionality and timely employee support across the organization.

### Key Responsibilities
'HRMS Administration'

- Maintain shift schedules in HRMS
- Maintain and update employee records accurately
- Support HRMS troubleshooting and employee system issues

'Employee Services'

- Create and manage employee access for Email, Lark, and ByteHR
- Support employee onboarding access setup and system activation
- Assist employees with HRMS, Email, and Lark login/access issues
- Respond to employee inquiries related to HR systems and attendance

'HR Operations Support'

- Support HR operational documentation and record management
- Coordinate with internal departments on employee system access matters
- Ensure employee information and HR records are updated accurately

### Requirements
- Bachelor's degree (any discipline).
- Minimum 2 years experience in HR operations or HRMS administration
- Good organizational and problem-solving skills
- Strong communication and coordination abilities
- Able to multitask and manage employee support requests efficiently
- Detail-oriented with good record management skills

### Benefits & Perks
- Working time: 9AM - 6PM (Mon-Fri)
- Fully remote work
- Performance review twice a year
- Opportunities for training and career growth
- Fast-paced, supportive work environment

### Note

Position and salary will be offered based on experience and skill level.
