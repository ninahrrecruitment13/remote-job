# Finance Controller
`Full-time · Remote · Salary: negotiable, discussed at interview.`

### About the role
We are a technology company building scalable digital platforms and operational systems for global clients. We operate with a strong focus on product excellence, engineering quality, speed of execution, and operational clarity.

As we continue to scale, we are building a lean, modern finance function from the ground up and are looking for a Controller to own the integrity of our books. This role exists to ensure our financial records are accurate, timely and compliant, so leadership can make decisions on numbers they trust.

The Controller oversees month-end close, internal controls, tax and compliance, and coordination with our outsourced partners. In this early stage, you will also cover treasury - making sure the business always has the right cash at the right time. You set the controls and processes that let the business scale with confidence.

This is a hands-on, founding role for someone who can build structure from scratch - designing the framework while staying close enough to the detail to ensure every number is right.

### Responsibilities
- Own the monthly, quarterly and annual close of the accounting books - running a fast, repeatable close calendar
- Prepare and review financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow), including balance sheet reconciliations, accruals, prepayments and revenue recognition reviews
- Deliver monthly management reporting and variance analysis to leadership
- Design, document and maintain the internal control framework - approval matrices, segregation of duties and payment controls
- Build and enforce finance and accounting policies and procedures; identify control gaps and financial risks, and drive remediation
- Support annual statutory audits and act as the main point of contact for external auditors
- Manage outsourced partners - set clear deliverables, deadlines and quality standards, and review their output
- Advise the Finance Manager / BOD on which activities to keep outsourced and which to bring in-house
- Cover treasury in the early stage - manage forecast and actual cash flow, oversee payment activities, and maintain banking relationships and payment providers

### Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field; CPA, ACCA, CA or equivalent professional qualification
- 6+ years of accounting experience, including 2+ years in a Controller, Financial Controller or senior Accounting Manager role; Big 4 audit background is a plus
- Hands-on experience running month-end close and consolidation for a multi-entity, multi-currency group
- Experience managing outsourced partners
- Working knowledge of cash management, banking and FX
- Experience in a fast-growing tech, SaaS, gaming or digital marketing company, and with cloud ERP/accounting systems (e.g. NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks Online), is a plus
- Fluent written and spoken English

### What Success Looks Like
- Books close on time every month, on a fast and predictable calendar
- Financial statements and management reports are accurate, timely and trusted by leadership
- Statutory audits complete smoothly, with no material adjustments or findings
- A clear internal control framework is in place - approvals, segregation of duties and payment controls are followed across the business
- Tax filings and regulatory obligations are met on time, with no penalties
- Outsourced partners deliver to clear standards and deadlines
- Cash position is always visible and forecast with confidence; payments run smoothly
